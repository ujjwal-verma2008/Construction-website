Who We Are

MAHAKAL BUILDCON (Traders and Construction) brings building construction, electrical solutions and mobile tower infrastructure together under one company, so clients deal with a single team from planning to handover.

We work on residential, commercial and industrial buildings, electrical installation and wiring, and mobile tower installation and maintenance. Quality materials, safe working practices, clear coordination and customer satisfaction guide how every project is carried out.
